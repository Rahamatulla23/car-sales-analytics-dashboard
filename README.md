# Car Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Car Sales Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using Power BI to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, dealer efficiency, and salesperson productivity.

The dashboard provides decision-makers with real-time insights into sales trends, revenue performance, customer preferences, and operational efficiency through interactive visualizations and KPI monitoring.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance and revenue trends.
* Identify top-performing vehicle models and regions.
* Evaluate dealer performance and operational efficiency.
* Understand customer behavior and purchasing patterns.
* Track salesperson productivity and performance metrics.
* Support data-driven business decision-making.
